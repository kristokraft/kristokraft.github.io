# Kristokraft DJ site

## Put it online (GitHub Pages, free)

1. Sign up at github.com. If you pick the username `kristokraft`, your site address becomes kristokraft.github.io.
2. Click **+ > New repository**. Name it `kristokraft.github.io` (use your exact username before `.github.io`). Set it to Public. Create.
3. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in `index.html`, `README.md` and the `images` folder. Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings > Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", pick **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`. Save.
5. Wait a minute or two, then open https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io

## Edit it

Open `index.html` on GitHub, click the pencil icon, and change the `SITE` section at the top:

- `bio`: your bio paragraphs
- `tracks`: SoundCloud track links (add more separated by commas)
- `instagramVideos`: links to public reels, e.g. "https://www.instagram.com/reel/ABC123/"
- `email`: your booking email
- `gigs`: upcoming dates, e.g. { date: "Oct 12", venue: "Venue", city: "Brooklyn", link: "" }

Click **Commit changes**. The site updates in about a minute.

## New photos

Upload them into the `images` folder, then add them to the `photos` list. The first photo is the big one at the top.
